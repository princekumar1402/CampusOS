# CampusOS Internship and Career Policy (MVP Policy Example)

## Overview
The CampusOS Internship & Career module connects undergraduate and graduate students with verified professional opportunities and industrial training.

## Internship Eligibility and Application Rules
1. Eligibility: Students in eligible academic terms with active student status can explore and apply for internship postings.
2. Skill Matching System:
   - Internship listings define required technical and professional skills (such as Python, SQL, React, Machine Learning, Fastapi).
   - CampusOS computes an explainable skill match percentage comparing the student's acquired skills with the posting requirements.
   - Students can review their matching skills and missing skills before applying.
3. Application Process:
   - Applications are submitted through the CampusOS Internship portal.
   - Applicants provide their portfolio link, resume URL, and statement of interest.
   - Duplicate applications for the same opening are strictly prohibited.
   - Students can monitor their application history and status from the My Applications dashboard.

## Career Conduct and Reporting
- Students who receive an official internship offer must notify the Career and Placement cell and verify that work hours do not conflict with mandatory class schedules.
