# CampusOS CampusFix Issue Reporting Policy (MVP Policy Example)

## Overview
CampusFix is the centralized university complaint and facility issue resolution system designed to maintain campus infrastructure and student amenities.

## Procedure for Reporting Issues
1. Submission: Students and campus members can lodge maintenance and facility complaints through the CampusFix portal on the CampusOS dashboard.
2. Complaint Categories:
   - Maintenance: Structural repairs, plumbing, furniture, and general campus facilities.
   - Electrical: Lighting, power outlets, circuit breakers, and laboratory power fixtures.
   - IT: Wi-Fi connectivity, lab workstations, network access points, and system errors.
   - Other: General campus amenities and custodial services.
3. Priority Levels:
   - Issues can be designated with urgency levels: Low, Medium, High, or Urgent.

## Complaint Resolution Workflow
- Open: The complaint has been registered and is pending initial review by campus facility staff.
- In Progress: A maintenance technician or IT personnel has been assigned and work is actively underway.
- Resolved: Corrective actions have been completed and verified.
- Status Notifications: The reporting user receives real-time notifications in their CampusOS Notification Center whenever the complaint status transitions.
