# CampusOS Events and Clubs Policy (MVP Policy Example)

## Overview
Campus events and student organizations foster community engagement, leadership development, and extracurricular learning.

## Campus Event Registration Procedure
1. Browsing Events: Upcoming workshops, hackathons, guest lectures, and cultural fests are cataloged in the CampusOS Events section.
2. Registering:
   - Any registered student can sign up for an active event with available seats.
   - Upon successful registration, the student receives an event confirmation and ticket record.
   - If unable to attend, students can unregister prior to the event start time to free up seating.
3. Event Creation: Authorized faculty and club administrators can create and manage event details, capacity, venue, and timings.

## Student Clubs and Societies
1. Membership:
   - Students may explore technical societies, arts collectives, and student clubs via the Clubs directory.
   - Joining a club adds the student to the active membership roster.
   - A student can leave a club at any time from their club membership dashboard.
2. Club Governance:
   - Official club creation requires authorization from campus administration or faculty advisors.
   - All student members are expected to uphold the university student code of conduct during club operations.
