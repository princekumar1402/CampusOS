# CampusOS Academic Guidelines and Student Code (MVP Policy Example)

## Overview
These academic guidelines govern student enrollment, course registration, grading policies, and scholastic integrity at CampusOS.

## Course Registration and Academic Credits
1. Course Enrollment:
   - Students register for required core courses and electives offered by their respective academic departments at the beginning of each semester.
   - Course listings specify course codes, department affiliations, credit units, prerequisites, and assigned faculty instructors.
2. Add/Drop Period:
   - Students may adjust course schedules during the designated add/drop registration window without academic penalty.
3. Credit Load:
   - Full-time students must maintain the prescribed minimum credit hours per semester according to their program curriculum.

## Grading System and CGPA
- Performance evaluation includes continuous assessments, mid-term examinations, and final semester evaluations.
- Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is computed based on weighted credit grades across all completed academic semesters.
- Students must maintain satisfactory academic standing according to departmental standards.

## Scholastic Honesty and Code of Conduct
- Plagiarism, unauthorized examination collaboration, and fraudulent submission of academic work are serious violations.
- Violations are subject to disciplinary review and penalties ranging from assignment failure to course debarment.
