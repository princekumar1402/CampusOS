# CampusOS Attendance Policy (MVP Policy Example)

## Overview
Regular attendance in scheduled academic sessions is essential for student learning and academic performance across all departments.

## Attendance Requirement
1. Minimum Requirement: Students are required to maintain a minimum attendance threshold of 75% in each registered course to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
2. Attendance Status Classification:
   - Good Standing: Attendance percentage at or above 75%.
   - Warning Status: Attendance percentage between 65% and 74%.
   - Critical Status: Attendance percentage below 65%.
3. Debarment: Students falling below 75% without authorized condonation may be debarred from taking final examinations.

## Recording and Tracking Attendance
- Faculty members record attendance for each course session through the CampusOS Attendance module.
- Students can view their attendance logs, current percentage, and required classes needed to reach 75% on their student dashboard.
- Attendance records cannot be marked or modified directly by students.

## Medical and Authorized Absence Condonation
- In exceptional circumstances such as documented illness or university-approved event participation, attendance condonation may be granted down to 65% upon timely submission of official medical or event certificates to the department.
